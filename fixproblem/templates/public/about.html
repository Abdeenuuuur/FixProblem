About Fixproblem

Fixproblem helps support managers and product teams see which customer problems keep coming back, notice when demand changes, and agree what to fix first, backed by the customers’ own words.

It is designed and built by Abdennour Al Ayoubi, a Computer Science graduate and MSc Business and Digital Transformation student at UWE Bristol, as a portfolio project. It is in an early pilot stage: the changelog and the README list exactly what is implemented and what is not.
