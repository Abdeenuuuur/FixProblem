Changelog

Fixproblem 2.3: review fixes

  • Payments are verified for the right account, amount and product; webhooks are signature-checked and processed once.
  • CSRF protection on every form; production refuses insecure defaults.
  • Account deletion removes all workspace files; data export includes workspace records.
  • Reports separate period, current and all-time figures; the PDF is robust to long or unusual text.
  • Readable issue titles, and issue workflow that follows the problem across re-analysis.
  • Honest pricing: one user per plan for now; Team is a one-month pass with no automatic renewal.

Fixproblem 2.2: assistant and easier start

  • The Fixproblem assistant on every page: grounded answers with sources, workspace questions and one-click actions, with optional Claude, OpenAI or Gemini.
  • A simpler home page with a question box and three quick-start steps.
  • Double-click start files for Mac and Windows.

Fixproblem 2.1: private workspaces

  • Every account gets its own private workspace, stored in its own database file.
  • A separate, clearly labelled sample workspace and a workspace switcher.
  • A starter model for new workspaces without labelled tickets.
  • Isolation tests for guessed IDs, search, exports, API, switching and tampered sessions.

Fixproblem 2.0 (Stage 1 and part of Stage 2)

  • New name, brand and accessible design system with light and dark themes.
  • Public website, interactive demo without registration, pricing, evaluation and help centre.
  • Guided CSV import: upload, map columns, validate, confirm, analyse; downloadable error report.
  • Issue investigation: description, priority explanation, evidence, “data shows / system suggests / team confirmed”, related issues, CSV export.
  • Review queue: reasons, skip and undo.
  • Trends with table alternatives; management report as HTML, CSV and PDF; before/after observations.
  • Feedback and contact forms with reference numbers.

1.x (SupportIQ)

  • Classification, issue discovery, emerging-issue alerts, issue board, review queue, accounts, plans and owner analytics.
