More tutorials

Feedback is everywhere. Patterns are easy to miss.

Complaints arrive across email, chat and phone, and each agent sees only their own queue. By the time a monthly spreadsheet shows a recurring problem, customers have already contacted you several times about it.

Scattered

The same problem is described in hundreds of different ways by different customers.

Inconsistent tags

Manual categories vary between agents, so reports undercount real issues.

Late

New problems surface weeks after they start, when they are already expensive.

What the AI does, and where people decide

The system

  • Suggests a category for each ticket, with a confidence score.
  • Groups similar tickets into recurring issues.
  • Flags statistically unusual increases in demand.
  • Ranks issues by volume, severity, customer tier and growth.

Your team

  • Reviews tickets the model is unsure about; corrections are recorded and can be undone.
  • Confirms root causes. A spike shows that demand changed, not why.
  • Decides priorities, owners and actions.
  • Optional external AI summaries are off unless an admin adds an API key.
