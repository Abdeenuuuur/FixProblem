Privacy notice

Pilot notice written with UK GDPR principles in mind. It describes what this version actually does.

What we collect

Account details (name, work email, workspace name), plan and payment records, whether you agreed to product emails, and a record of what you do in the app (pages, actions, time). Demo visitors are recorded with a random ID. Contact and support forms store what you send us.

What we never collect

Your password in readable form (we store a one-way hash) or card details (handled by Stripe). Diagnostics are attached to support requests only if you tick the box, and never include ticket text.

Customer ticket data

Tickets you import are used only to provide the analysis. Optional external AI summaries are off unless an admin adds an API key; when on, recent ticket text for an alert is sent to that provider.

Your rights

From My account you can download your data or delete your account. You can also contact us, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Retention

Activity records up to 12 months; payment records 6 years for tax purposes; support requests until resolved plus 12 months.
