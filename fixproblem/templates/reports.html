Methodology and limitations

Categories come from a TF-IDF and logistic-regression model; issues from k-means groups within each category; alerts from a Poisson test with Bonferroni correction (last 14 days vs the previous 8 weeks). Alerts show changes in demand, not causes. Team-confirmed findings are shown separately from system output. See the evaluation page.
